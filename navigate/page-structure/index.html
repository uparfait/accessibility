Guide/Navigate

Page structure

Shows a list of the parts of the page, so you can jump straight to one.

Lists

  • Headings: the titles of each section
  • Regions: main areas such as the menu and the footer
  • Links: every link on the page

How to use

  1. Choose a list at the top
  2. Choose an item to jump to it

Use it from your own code

This one has no setting, because it is something visitors open and read. Your page can open the panel for them:

Accessibles.open();

Try it yourself

Open the panel, choose the Navigate tab, then Page structure.
