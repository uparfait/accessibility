Guide

Text

These features change how the words on the page look: their size, letters, spacing and direction.

In this tab

  • Size, font and spacing
  • Alignment and page direction
  • Highlights for headings and links
  • A magnifier for small text

Good to know

  • Buttons with small bars have several levels. Press again for the next level

The features

Content scalingMakes the whole page bigger: text, pictures and buttons together.Bigger textMakes only the text bigger. Pictures and layout stay the same.FontChanges the letters used on the whole page.Line heightAdds space between lines of text, so your eyes can follow each line.Letter spacingAdds space between letters, so they do not run together.Word spacingAdds space between words, so each word stands on its own.Bolder textMakes thin text thicker and darker.Text alignChanges how lines of text line up.Text directionChanges the direction of the whole page.Highlight titlesDraws a box around every heading, so you can see how the page is organized at a glance.Highlight linksUnderlines and outlines every link, so you always know what you can click.Text magnifierShows the text under your pointer in large letters next to it.
