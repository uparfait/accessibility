Reading mask

Darkens the page above and below your pointer and leaves a clear band where you read.

Good for

  • Focusing on a few lines at a time

Use it from your own code

The setting is called readingMask. Use true to turn it on and false to turn it off.

Accessibles.set('readingMask', true);    // on
Accessibles.set('readingMask', false);   // off
Accessibles.get('readingMask');          // is it on?

Use the switch above the sample page to turn Reading mask on and off.
