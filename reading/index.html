Reading

These features help people read and understand the page, by listening, focusing or simplifying.

In this tab

  • Read aloud, reading guide and reading mask
  • Stop animations, hide images and mute sounds
  • Tooltips, reader view, dictionary and summary
  • Sign language and translation
  • Reading language, voice, speed and pitch, at the bottom

The features

Read aloudReads the page out loud.Reading guideShows a line under your pointer that moves with you, to help you keep your place while reading.Reading maskDarkens the page above and below your pointer and leaves a clear band where you read.Stop animationsStops everything that moves: animations, moving pictures, slideshows and videos that play by themselves.Hide imagesHides pictures and background images, so you can focus on the text.Mute soundsTurns off all sound on the page, including videos.TooltipsShows the hidden name or description of buttons, links and pictures in a small bubble when you point at them or select them.Spoken tipsShort spoken help when you use a sound feature.Reader viewShows the main text of the page in a clean, simple view.DictionaryShows what a word means.Page summaryGives you the main points of the page in a few sentences.Sign languageShows the page in sign language.TranslateTranslates the whole page, and this menu, into your language.Reading voiceChoose how read aloud sounds.
