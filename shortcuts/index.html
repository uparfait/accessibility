Keyboard and voice shortcuts

Everything in the panel works with a keyboard, and most of the page can be used by voice. Here is every shortcut in one place.

Keys

KeysWhat they do
Alt + AOpen or close the panel
EscClose the open window, view or panel, and stop reading
Alt + RRead the page, or pause and go on, when read aloud is on
Alt + N, Alt + Shift + NNext or previous paragraph, when read aloud is on
Alt + H, L, F, B, M, GNext heading, link, field, button, region or image, when keyboard navigation is on. Add Shift to go back.
Alt + arrow keysMove the round button while it has focus

Voice commands

Turn on Voice commands and allow the microphone. Commands don't need exact words: "scrol down" works too, and when the widget isn't sure it asks "Did you mean…?"

SayOr
Scroll down, Scroll upGo to top, Go to bottom
Click + a name, such as "Click Contact"Show numbers, then say a number
Type + words, such as "Type anna at mail dot com"Next field, Previous field, Clear field
Press Enter, or "Submit"Turn on or Turn off + a name, such as "Turn on dark contrast"
Choose + an option, such as "Choose Membership"Search for + words
Go to + a website, such as "Go to wikipedia dot org"Next heading, Previous heading, Next link
Go back, Go forward, ReloadOpen menu, Close menu
Read page, Read this, Read headingsPause, Continue, Next, Previous, Faster, Slower
StopWhat can I say, Stop listening

Talk & type

Click into a text box and turn on Talk & type. What you say is typed. These words do something instead:

SayOr
Period, Comma, Question mark, Colon, DashOpen quote, Close quote, Open bracket, Close bracket
New line, New paragraphDelete that, Delete first word, Delete last word
Delete last 3 wordsDelete + a word, such as "Delete Tuesday"
Clear from start, Clear to end, Clear allGo to next input, Go to previous input
Press Enter, SendStop, What can I say, Stop typing

In email fields, "anna at mail dot com" becomes anna@mail.com.

A whole task by voice

With these commands a visitor can sign in, search, fill in a form and pay without a mouse. For example:

  1. "Click Log in"
  2. "Type anna at mail dot com", then "Next field", then "Type" and the password
  3. "Turn on remember me", then "Press Enter"
  4. "Search for blue shoes", then "Click Add to cart", then "Choose Size 40"
  5. "Click Checkout", fill in the form the same way, then "Submit"

When it isn't sure

A question bar appears at the top of the screen, with buttons. Every answer can also be spoken:

  • Did you mean…? Say "Yes" or "No". Opening another website always asks first.
  • Several matches, such as three Delete buttons. They are all outlined, and the current one is marked "2 / 3". Say "Yes", "Next", "Previous", a number, "All" or "Stop".

Saying "Stop" or pressing Esc ends reading, a question or a choice at any time.
