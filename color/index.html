Color

These features change the colors of the page, from a dark theme to fixes for color blindness.

In this tab

  • Dark, light, high and smart contrast
  • Inverted colors and saturation
  • Corrections for color blindness
  • Your own colors for text, titles and background
  • Brightness

The features

Dark modeTurns the whole page dark but keeps its own colors: every background becomes dark and every text becomes light, each in its own color.Dark contrastShows light text on a dark background.Light contrastShows dark text on a plain white background.High contrastMakes the difference between light and dark parts stronger, so text and edges stand out.Smart contrastFixes only the text that is hard to read because its color is too close to the background.Invert colorsSwaps light and dark colors on the page.MonochromeShows the page in black, white and gray, which removes color distractions.Low saturationMakes colors softer and calmer.High saturationMakes colors stronger and brighter. This can help if colors look faded to you.Color blindnessCorrects the page colors for color blindness, so colors that look alike become easier to tell apart.Custom colorsChoose your own colors for text, titles and the page background.BrightnessMakes the whole page darker or lighter.
