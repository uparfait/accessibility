Accessibility tools for any website

Accessibles adds a small button to your site. Visitors use it to make text bigger, change colors, stop animations, hear the page read aloud or move around by voice. It also adds missing labels and image descriptions to the page, without any changes to your HTML.

Add it to your site →See the demo page

Two lines are enough

Paste these lines before the closing </body> tag of your page. A round button appears in the corner, and the panel opens when you click it or press Alt + A.

<div class="accessibles-widget"></div>
<script src="https://uparfait.github.io/accessibility/v1/widgets/accessibles.js" defer></script>

Here they are on a small sample page. Click the round button in the preview and try a few features.

What visitors get

The panel has six tabs. Each one gathers features for one kind of need.

ProfilesOne-click setups for vision, dyslexia, ADHD, seizures and more.TextSize, fonts, spacing, alignment and right-to-left pages.ColorDark, light and smart contrast, color blindness, your own colors.NavigateKeyboard, big cursors, on-screen keyboard and voice control.ReadingRead aloud, reading mask, reader view, dictionary and translation.ToolsAutomatic repairs, page check, reports and settings.

Fixes it makes on its own

Many sites have buttons without names, pictures without descriptions and form fields without labels. Screen readers can't explain those to their users. Accessibles finds them and fills in what is missing as the page loads, and keeps doing it for content that appears later. Nothing changes for people who don't use a screen reader. Read more in ARIA fixes, Image descriptions and Form fixes.
