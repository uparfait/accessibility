New this season

The reading room has been refurbished with brighter lighting, larger tables and quieter shelving. A new accessible entrance on the river side of the building now has step-free access, automatic doors and a lowered service desk. Large-print books and audiobooks have moved to the ground floor so they are easier to reach.

We have also added two computers with screen magnifiers and headphones. Staff can help you set them up, and you can book a computer for up to two hours a day. Printing is available at the front desk for a small charge.
